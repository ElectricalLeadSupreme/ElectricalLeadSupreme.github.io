What We Do

Production Setup
Consulting Services

We specialize in the full lifecycle of machine shop setup — from first article to production-ready. Every engagement is hands-on, not advisory.

01

New Part Setup

We analyze your print, select tooling, write or review your program, and verify the first article — so your machinists are not troubleshooting from scratch.

02

Cycle Time Reduction

We audit existing setups for wasted motion, suboptimal feeds and speeds, redundant operations, and fixturing inefficiencies — then make the changes with you.

03

Workholding & Fixturing

Custom and modular fixturing solutions designed for repeatability, fast changeover, and consistent tolerance on complex geometries.

04

Setup Documentation

We create clear, photo-referenced setup sheets your operators can follow without calling the engineer — reducing errors and onboarding time.

05

Machinist Training

Structured on-machine training focused on setup efficiency, tool management, and reading GD&T — practical skills that stick.

06

Production Launch

We help transition jobs from first article to repeatable production with documented processes, operator checks, and stable handoff routines.
