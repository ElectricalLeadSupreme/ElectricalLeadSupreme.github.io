What We Do

Production Setup
Consulting Services

We specialize in the full lifecycle of machine shop setup — from first article to production-ready. Every engagement is hands-on, not advisory.

New Part Setup

We analyze your print, select tooling, write or review your program, and verify the first article — so your machinists aren't troubleshooting from scratch.

Cycle Time Reduction

We audit existing setups for wasted motion, suboptimal feeds and speeds, redundant operations, and fixturing inefficiencies — then make the changes with you.

Workholding & Fixturing

Custom and modular fixturing solutions designed for repeatability, fast changeover, and consistent tolerance on complex geometries.

Setup Documentation

We create clear, photo-referenced setup sheets your operators can follow without calling the engineer — reducing errors and onboarding time.

Machinist Training

Structured on-machine training focused on setup efficiency, tool management, and reading GD&T — practical skills that stick because they're learned on your own equipment.

Production Launch Support

On-site during your production launch to catch issues in real time, not after they show up in your scrap rate. We stay until the process is stable.

How It Works

A Structured Engagement,
Not an Open Tab

Every project follows a defined scope with a clear end state. You'll know exactly what we're doing and when we're done.

01

Shop Assessment

We spend a day on your floor reviewing current setups, your job mix, equipment, and where time and quality are being lost. No charge for the first visit.

02

Scope & Proposal

We write a plain-language proposal: what we'll tackle, what you'll have when we're done, timeline, and fixed-fee pricing. No hourly billing surprises.

03

On-Site Execution

We work beside your team on your machines. Changes are made, tested, and documented in real time — not handed off as recommendations.

04

Handoff & Follow-Up

You receive complete setup documentation and a 30-day follow-up check. If something drifts, we come back and fix it.
